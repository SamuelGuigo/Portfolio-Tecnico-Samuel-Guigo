# Data Center Rack & Cabling Infrastructure Planning

Sanitized professional case based on a corporate data-center / CPD reorganization project led technically as part of my role at **EGE Soluções**.

## Scope

I worked on a physical infrastructure project involving multiple racks, network equipment, servers, firewalls, telecom devices, patch panels, fiber distribution and structured cabling.

## My responsibilities

- field survey and technical inventory;
- identification of equipment and connections;
- asset labeling;
- patch-panel and switch-port mapping;
- connection tracing;
- rack-layout planning;
- inter-rack connectivity planning;
- cable-routing and maintenance-access planning;
- material requirements;
- execution sequencing;
- maintenance-window planning;
- technical documentation;
- rollback and validation planning.

## Design principles

The project emphasized:

- clean and maintainable front-facing rack organization;
- predictable cable routing;
- maintenance through patch panels;
- separation of planning from as-built documentation;
- minimizing production risk during physical changes;
- documenting unresolved connections before disconnecting anything.

## Delivery discipline

The project included partial field execution and progressive inventory improvement, but I do not present the final rack layout or customer acceptance as completed unless supported by final evidence.
